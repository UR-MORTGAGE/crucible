SOURCE: Fannie Mae Eligibility Matrix
# Maximum LTV Purchase
The maximum loan-to-value (LTV) ratio for a one-unit principal residence purchase is 97%. LTV is the loan amount divided by the lesser of the sales price or the appraised value.

# Cash-out Refinance LTV
The maximum LTV for a cash-out refinance on a one-unit principal residence is 80%. Excess cash back on a rate-and-term refinance beyond the allowable limit requires the loan to be restructured or re-disclosed.

# Down Payment and Gift Funds
The borrower may satisfy the required down payment and closing costs with their own funds or eligible gift funds from a related person, documented with a gift letter and evidence of transfer.
