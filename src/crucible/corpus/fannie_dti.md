# Maximum DTI Ratio
The maximum allowable debt-to-income (DTI) ratio for loans underwritten through Desktop Underwriter is 50%. The DTI ratio is the borrower's total monthly obligations, including the proposed housing payment, divided by stable monthly qualifying income.

# Qualifying Payment
The qualifying monthly housing expense (PITIA) includes principal, interest, property taxes, insurance, and any HOA or assessment dues. All monthly liabilities appearing on the credit report with more than ten remaining payments must be included in the DTI.

# Temporary Buydowns
A temporary interest rate buydown reduces the borrower's initial payment. When a buydown is used, the borrower is qualified at the note rate, not the bought-down rate, unless the transaction meets the specific eligibility for qualifying at the reduced rate.
