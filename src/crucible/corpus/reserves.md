# Reserve Requirements
Reserves are measured as the number of months of the qualifying housing payment (PITIA) that the borrower has in liquid assets after closing. The required amount is determined by Desktop Underwriter based on the loan and property characteristics.

# Eligible Reserve Assets
Eligible reserves include vested funds in checking, savings, money market, and retirement accounts. Funds required for down payment and closing costs may not be counted toward reserves.
