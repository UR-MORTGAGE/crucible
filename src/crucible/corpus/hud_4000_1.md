SOURCE: HUD Handbook 4000.1
# FHA Maximum DTI Ratio
For FHA-insured mortgages, qualifying ratios may exceed 43% with documented compensating factors, up to a maximum total DTI of 56.99% for borrowers with strong compensating factors under TOTAL Scorecard.

# FHA Maximum LTV
The maximum LTV for an FHA purchase transaction is 96.5% for borrowers with a minimum decision credit score of 580 or higher. Borrowers with a decision score between 500 and 579 are limited to 90% LTV.

# FHA Minimum Credit Score
A minimum decision credit score of 580 is required for maximum financing. The decision credit score is the middle of three scores or the lower of two.

# FHA Reserve Requirements
Reserves are not required for 1-2 unit properties under FHA TOTAL Scorecard approval. Three months of reserves are required for 3-4 unit properties.

# FHA Temporary Buydowns
Temporary interest rate buydowns are permitted on purchase transactions; the borrower must be qualified at the note rate. The buydown agreement must be executed by all parties and included in the case binder.
