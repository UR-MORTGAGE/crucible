SOURCE: Fannie Mae Selling Guide B3-5.1
# Minimum Credit Score
The minimum representative credit score for a conventional loan underwritten through Desktop Underwriter is 620. The representative score is the middle of three or the lower of two scores.

# Bankruptcy Waiting Period
A Chapter 7 bankruptcy requires a waiting period of 4 years (48 months) from the discharge or dismissal date. A Chapter 13 bankruptcy requires 2 years from the discharge date. A shorter period may apply only with documented extenuating circumstances.

# Foreclosure Waiting Period
A prior foreclosure requires a waiting period of 7 years (84 months) from the completion date. A deed-in-lieu or short sale requires 4 years.

# Judgments and Liens
Open judgments, garnishments, and outstanding liens against the borrower or the subject property must be paid in full or subordinated prior to or at closing. Evidence of payoff or a recorded subordination agreement is required.

# Credit Inquiries and Undisclosed Debt
Credit inquiries within the most recent 120 days require a borrower letter of explanation stating whether new credit was obtained. Any resulting new debt must be included in the DTI.
